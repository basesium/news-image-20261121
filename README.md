# The World — 21 November 2026

A single-image summary of the world's news on 21 November 2026, drawn as the front page of a newspaper called THE WORLD. One glance carries the whole day: the capture, the Gulf strikes, the markets and the disasters that defined it.

## What's inside

- Mexico captures "El Chapo Isidro", the FBI most-wanted cartel boss, in a Sinaloa raid; 11 killed.
- Iran hits U.S. bases in Bahrain and tankers in the Strait of Hormuz.
- A Russian missile hits Zelenskyy's hometown, Kryvyi Rih: 2 killed, 60 wounded.
- The S&P 500 drops 3.7% and the Nikkei 5.1% in an AI stock sell-off.
- COP31 climate talks in Antalya run past their deadline as the draft drops fossil fuels.
- A 6.8-magnitude earthquake shakes the Banda Sea off Timor-Leste.
- A boat capsizes in Nigeria, killing 24 people on their way to a funeral.

## How to run

Run the program with python3 main.py. It copies entry.png into an /output directory, creating the folder first when it is missing.

The entry image is entry.png, 1024x1024.
